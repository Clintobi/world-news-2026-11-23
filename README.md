## What it is
A single 1024×1024 image (JPEG-encoded, written to entry.png as the brief allows) summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026, designed as a magazine cover: a full-bleed hero of the Hormuz tanker attacks, an editorial band with three pictured stories, and a breadth line:

- **Lead:** Tankers in flames in the Strait of Hormuz — at least three oil tankers struck, two more set ablaze after hitting naval mines; attacks on shipping at their highest since the war began (AP · CNN-UKMTO · IRNA)
- **COP31 talks collapse** — longest-ever UN climate talks end in Turkey with no deal (BBC · NYT)
- **Russian warship sunk** — suspected Ukrainian sea drones hit the corvette Merkury at Benghazi (AP · Reuters)
- **Boy, 7, pulled alive** from rubble after a Russian strike near Kyiv (BBC · Al Jazeera)
- **Democrats' House surge** — largest House majority since 1992 after flipping a North Carolina seat (BBC)
- **Mercury, at last** — ESA/JAXA BepiColombo enters Mercury orbit after an 8-year voyage (ESA)
- **Lahore chokes: AQI 538** — world's most polluted major city (IQAir · Dawn)
- Breadth line: COP31 collapses in Turkey · Boy, 7, pulled alive near Kyiv · Democrats' largest US House majority since 1992 · AI rout in Asia

## How it runs
`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies `entry_image.png` to `/output/entry.png`. Deterministic — identical bytes on every run.
